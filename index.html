English and Turkish messages are welcome. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, game version, and what happened. A screenshot can help. Please do not send passwords or payment card details.

My ferry tipped over

Check the failure explanation and choose Adjust Layout to return to your pre-departure arrangement. Try moving a heavy load closer to the center or adjusting the side that carried too much weight.

Weight distribution is only part of the challenge: watch the tilt indicator during the waves.

Purchases and helper boats

If a purchase is missing, contact support with the product name and purchase date. Apple handles billing; you can request a refund through Apple’s purchase support.

Use the in-game restore option for eligible support purchases. Consumed helper boats cannot be restored through that option.

Ads and privacy choices

An ad may be unavailable because of your connection or ad availability. Try again later.

When available, open Privacy Choices in the game to review your advertising consent. Read our privacy policy for details.

Reminders and saved progress

Manage reminders in your iPhone’s Settings under Notifications → Tippico.

Progress is saved on your device. Removing the app can remove that progress. Contact support before reinstalling if you are troubleshooting a problem.
