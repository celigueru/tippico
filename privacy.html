Privacy policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Who we are

Tippico is an iOS cargo-balancing game provided by Atakan Yılmaz. This policy describes the game, its advertising and purchase services, and this support website. Contact: atakanyilmazz00@outlook.com.

Game data on your device

The game saves level progress, stars, cargo layouts, settings, energy, helper boat balances, and purchase-related records locally to operate the game and prevent duplicate rewards. You do not need to create a Tippico account. The current game does not provide a developer-operated cloud save service.

Advertising

Tippico uses Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform to show ads and manage advertising consent. Google’s SDK may process IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from them), device or advertising identifiers where available, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic and performance information. This supports advertising, measurement, and service operation.

Available advertising and data use depend on your consent, device permissions, region, and service settings. Where required, the game presents consent options. You can revisit them through Privacy Choices when that option is available. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily stop all ad-related data processing.

Read Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s iOS advertising data disclosure.

In-app purchases

Apple processes payments through the App Store. Tippico receives product and transaction information to deliver purchases, record ownership, and avoid duplicate delivery. We do not receive your payment card details. Eligible support purchases can be restored; consumable helper boats are handled separately. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Reminders

With your permission, Tippico schedules local reminders on your device. These reminders do not require a developer-operated push notification server. You can disable them in iOS notification settings.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to answer your request and investigate problems. Support email is handled through Microsoft Outlook. Avoid sending unnecessary personal information. See Microsoft’s Privacy Statement.

This website

This support site has no contact form and adds no advertising or analytics scripts. Its hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser information, to deliver and protect the site. Email links open your email application.

Retention and your choices

Local game data remains on your device until it is removed; device backups may retain a copy. Removing local data does not remove Apple’s transaction records or data held by service providers. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request, related issues, or applicable legal obligations.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, withdraw consent, or complain to a data protection authority. Email us to make a request about information we hold. For data held independently by Apple, Google, or Microsoft, their policies explain the available controls. Providers may process information outside your country.

Children

Tippico is a general-audience game. We do not ask players to provide a name, date of birth, or contact details to play. Parents or guardians who believe personal information about a child has been provided to us can contact us to request review and deletion.

Updates

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
